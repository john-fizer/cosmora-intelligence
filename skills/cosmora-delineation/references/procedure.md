# Cosmora natal delineation

## Inputs and sequence

This is a Cosmora editorial worksheet, not a claim that all ancient authors used one reading order. Required: verified birth instant, location, uncertainty, ephemeris identity, tropical longitudes, Whole Sign Ascendant, Sun altitude and motion/visibility data. Preserve the modern Whole Sign profile with Chiron and nodes separately; historical techniques use traditional rulers.

1. Freeze zodiac, house system, ruler table, aspect/orb policy and source edition. Record uncertainty in Ascendant, Lots and timing anchors.
2. Establish day/night sect from the geometric Sun horizon condition. House number alone is not a horizon test; require a stated refraction/twilight policy near the horizon.
3. Tabulate planet, longitude, Whole Sign house, house rulership and degree angularity. Treat Whole Sign angularity and proximity to an angle as distinct features.
4. Select the question/topic and its house. Trace its ruler to sign and house, then inspect the ruler's condition and relevant occupants. Record the full chain, including contradictory testimony.
5. Apply domicile/exaltation and the specifically named triplicity, bounds and face tables. Do not combine Egyptian and Ptolemaic bounds. Dorothean day/night/participating rulers: fire Sun/Jupiter/Saturn; earth Venus/Moon/Mars; air Saturn/Mercury/Jupiter; water Venus/Mars/Moon. Qualitative life thirds are not automatically dated periods.
6. Build sign configurations separately from degree aspects: conjunction/same sign, sextile/two signs, square/three, trine/four, opposition/six; otherwise aversion under this scheme. Application/separation requires actual motion and a named convention; do not infer it from angular distance alone.
7. Add named rules for speed, retrogradation, stations, solar visibility, reception and maltreatment/bonification only after documenting thresholds and exceptions. These catalog components remain research-only until formalized and checked.
8. Write bounded topic hypotheses with supporting and opposing features. Avoid deriving identities or retrospective stories from known biographies. Freeze the worksheet before event disclosure.
9. Add one named timing layer at a time: profections, releasing, returns, progressions or directions. Preserve the originating technique for every feature; agreement between correlated techniques is not independent evidence.

## Additional timing research

Primary directions, distributions through bounds and circumambulations require explicit significator/promissor, celestial geometry, latitude treatment, key and bounds table. Do not replace these with an unexplained one-degree-per-year rule. Decennials need verified starting and subdivision rules. Secondary progressions, solar arcs and returns require their own date, angle and location conventions. Firdaria is a later Persian technique, not an undifferentiated Hellenistic method. All remain research-only in this release; use the research skill to complete them.

Output: ordered testimony table, contradictions, topic hypotheses, timing layers and missing inputs. The worksheet is procedure-only; its component research statuses do not become executable because they are mentioned here.
