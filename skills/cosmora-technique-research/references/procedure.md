# Cosmora technique research

## Ordered research and implementation

1. Identify the method, original-language term, author/lineage, period and intended question. Record transliteration assumptions. Separate later innovations from ancient attributions.
2. Seek primary texts, originator publications or author-maintained technical documentation. Preserve edition, translator, book/chapter/sutra/page and URL. A feature list establishes existence, not an algorithm. Practitioner explanations may define a specifically named modern reconstruction.
3. Retrieve permitted content; record access date, precise locator and actual content hash when obtained. Preserve inaccessible sources and unresolved attribution as gaps. Never claim primary-text review or a hash that did not occur.
4. Specify prerequisites: coordinate system, time scale, location, houses, ruler/co-lord tables, ayanamsha, nodes, sect, age/year model, demographic inputs and uncertainty.
5. Extract the actual algorithm in execution order: normalization, complete tables, formulas, conditions, exception hierarchy, units, boundary inclusivity, loop/termination behavior and output schema. Link each rule to its source locator. Label editorial workflows as editorial.
6. Split conflicting schools into named, versioned methods. Do not borrow a missing tie breaker or subperiod rule silently from another lineage. Record omissions explicitly.
7. Hand-derive worked examples with intermediate values. Cover ordinary inputs, exact/near boundaries, invalid values, ties and uncertain birth times. Compare against independent published calculations or implementations; self-consistency alone does not verify astronomy.
8. Assign status: research_only for incomplete rules; procedure_only for ordered manual procedures lacking a complete engine; interpretation_only for symbolic concepts; implemented_arithmetic after executable reference checks. Source review and empirical validation remain separate.
9. Implement deterministic arithmetic separately from interpretation. Bind results to method/profile definitions, inputs and implementation hashes. Package SKILL.md, references/procedure.md, references/catalog.json and quoted UI metadata. Validate the package and exercise its public interface.
10. Have a fresh reviewer follow the procedure without unstated assumptions. Add method/source/profile nodes to the doctrine graph; keep observed events, calculated placements, interpretations and correlation hypotheses distinguishable.
11. For evaluation, anonymize charts, freeze rules and probabilistic predictions before outcome disclosure, prespecify topics/time windows/scoring and include baselines. Split by person/time to reduce leakage, preserve untouched holdouts and account for multiple searches. Revise on development cases and test on fresh cases. Agreement between related techniques is not independent evidence.

## Output and stopping conditions

Deliver a technique card, numbered procedure, complete tables or permitted references, worked examples, tests, sources and unresolved issues. Research-only methods cannot be executed as though complete. Continue supported calculations and useful research while marking missing rules. Arithmetic success and retrospective narrative fit do not establish predictive validity.

Current gaps include full bounds-table reconciliation, Chara Dasha variants/subperiods, Stojanovic indexing, native sidereal integration and Chinese calendar fixtures. BaZi is user-deferred and excluded from default technique selection; research it only on explicit request. The inventory is expandable rather than an exhaustive list of every historical technique.
