# Research protocol — 100 verified people

Pilot: 5 people, expand to 25 then 100 only after passing gates. Target 20–50 events/person is an aspiration, not a guarantee. Prefer AA-rated birth records, and document provenance and permissions. A/A/B may be explored in sensitivity analyses but not silently mixed with AA. No rectified birth times treated as verified.

Candidate discovery: Astro-Databank birth-record metadata (respect licensing and access), Wikidata entity discovery, official biographies, dated press releases, reputable archives, primary records. Multiple syndications are not independent sources. Do not scrape paywalled or disallowed material.

Separate date of occurrence, date of announcement, publication date, and inferred period. Precision is mandatory. Record selection bias, publicity coverage, profession and era. For sensitive life events, avoid speculative claims about living people.

For each event calculate natal features, transits, secondary progressions, annual profections, solar returns, zodiacal releasing where implementation and time precision permit. Generate matched non-event windows to prevent event-only training. Store complete calculation provenance.

Doctrine graph ingestion: public-domain, licensed or permitted books and reference material. Each rule requires bibliographic citation, exact source location, tradition, applicability conditions, exceptions and contradiction links. Distinguish textbook interpretations from empirically tested claims.

Experimental evaluation: predeclare targets and time windows, separate by person, check publication-time leakage, compare non-astrological baselines, correct multiple comparisons, use effect-size uncertainty and untouched replication cohort. No claims of predictive validity absent results.

Legal review: ephemeris license, text reuse, website terms, copyright, publicity/privacy and personal data handling before commercial deployment.
